Our Story

From a small team of educators to a global learning platform trusted by thousands.

From Humble Beginnings to Global Impact

IT Technologies was founded in 2018 with a simple belief: that anyone, regardless of background or location, deserves access to high-quality technology education. What started as a handful of recorded coding tutorials has grown into a platform with 250+ courses, expert instructors from around the world, and a community of more than 10,000 learners.

Today we partner with leading companies and industry professionals to deliver practical, project-based learning that prepares students for real careers in web development, data science, design, cybersecurity and beyond.
